**Português**
- Se o jogo fechar logo ao abrir com a Geração de Quadros (Streamline/DLSS-G), o port reabre sozinho sem ela e passa a abrir sem ela (apague user\framegen.off, ou defina SHADPS4_FG_FORCE=1, para tentar de novo). Quedas sem tratamento gravam user\log\crash.txt e crash.dmp, e o report do jogo leva o crash.txt e o entrada.txt.

**English**
- If the game closes right on start with Frame Generation (Streamline/DLSS-G), the port reopens it by itself without it and keeps opening without it (delete user\framegen.off, or set SHADPS4_FG_FORCE=1, to try again). Unhandled crashes write user\log\crash.txt and crash.dmp, and the game's report carries crash.txt and entrada.txt.

**Español**
- Si el juego se cierra al abrir con la Generación de Fotogramas (Streamline/DLSS-G), el port lo reabre solo sin ella y sigue abriendo sin ella (borra user\framegen.off, o define SHADPS4_FG_FORCE=1, para volver a probar). Los cierres sin tratar escriben user\log\crash.txt y crash.dmp, y el reporte del juego lleva crash.txt y entrada.txt.
