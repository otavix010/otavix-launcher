**Português**
- **Fotos dos jogos de volta:** o site público de onde vinham as capas e imagens de 18 jogos (os de PS2 e PSP, FIFA Street 2 e Silent Hill) saiu do ar. Agora elas vêm do servidor do Otavix Launcher, e God of War: Chains of Olympus e Ghost of Sparta ganham as suas fotos.

**English**
- **Game pictures are back:** the public site the covers and pictures of 18 games came from (the PS2 and PSP ones, FIFA Street 2 and Silent Hill) went offline. They now come from the Otavix Launcher's server, and God of War: Chains of Olympus and Ghost of Sparta get their pictures.

**Español**
- **Vuelven las fotos de los juegos:** el sitio público de donde venían las portadas e imágenes de 18 juegos (los de PS2 y PSP, FIFA Street 2 y Silent Hill) dejó de funcionar. Ahora vienen del servidor de Otavix Launcher, y God of War: Chains of Olympus y Ghost of Sparta reciben sus fotos.
