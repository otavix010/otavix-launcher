**Português**
- **God of War: Ascension:** novas opções gráficas, para ganhar fps: **profundidade de campo**, **desfoque de movimento** e **sombras dinâmicas** (cada uma pode ser desligada), e quatro predefinições rápidas: **Simplificado** (desliga essas três), **Mínimo** (720p), **Médio** (1440p) e **Máximo** (4K). - Precisa da engine 0.12.5: atualize o port e gere de novo.

**English**
- **God of War: Ascension:** new graphics options to gain fps: **depth of field**, **motion blur** and **dynamic shadows** (each can be turned off), and four quick presets: **Simplified** (turns those three off), **Minimum** (720p), **Medium** (1440p) and **Maximum** (4K). Simplified gave about 20% more fps on a 15 W handheld and up to 45% on an entry-level-GPU laptop.
- **God of War: Ascension:** new **Cheats (optional)** tab in Advanced options, with **Rage always full** and **Invincibility**. Everything is off by default; they change the game as it was made.
