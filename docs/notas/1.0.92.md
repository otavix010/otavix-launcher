**Português**
- **God of War: Ascension no Linux / SteamOS (beta):** o launcher agora gera e abre o God of War: Ascension no Linux. Escolha o disco (o update e o firmware são opcionais) e clique em gerar: o gerador confere as ferramentas que precisa e avisa o que faltar. Precisa de um Proton instalado na Steam e de uns 15 GB livres.

**English**
- **God of War: Ascension on Linux / SteamOS (beta):** the launcher now makes and opens God of War: Ascension on Linux. Pick the disc (the update and the firmware are optional) and click generate: the generator checks the tools it needs and tells you what is missing. It needs a Proton installed in Steam and about 15 GB free.
